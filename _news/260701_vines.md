I am very happy to announce that the project we prepared together with
Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), Stevens Institute of Technology,
and Nokia Bell Lab was just funded by Business Finland. The 3-year project,
which was submitted to the joint US/Finland VINES call (Track 2), aims to study
the application of foundational semantic communication to private networking
technologies, spatial AI, and robotics.

More info about the project can be found [here]({{ "funding/2026_vines/" | relative_url }}).
